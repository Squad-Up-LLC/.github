**SquadUp** is a social fitness app for iPhone. Find workout sessions happening near you, join a
squad, or host your own and get paid.

### Three ways to use SquadUp

- **Find local sessions.** Browse workouts near you and filter by type, time and location. Free and
  paid options.
- **Train together.** Join sessions, chat with your squad before, during and after, and build your
  fitness crew.
- **Host and get paid.** Go Elite to host paid sessions, set your own rates and keep up to 85% of
  what you earn. Climb the Elite ranks as you go.
